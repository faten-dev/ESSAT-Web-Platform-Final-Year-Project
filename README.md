**Degree:** Bachelor's Degree in Business Computing  
**Specialisation:** Business Information Systems

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of my final year of studies in Business Computing at ESSAT (École Supérieure des Sciences Appliquées et de la Technologie Privée de Gabès), Tunisia.

The objective was to design and develop a dynamic web platform to improve communication, information sharing and administrative management within the institution.

The project aimed to address limitations identified in the school's previous website, particularly the lack of interactive services for students, prospective students and administrative staff.

The proposed solution introduced a centralised platform providing different functionalities according to user roles.

## Project Objectives

- Improve communication between the institution and its stakeholders.
- Digitalise administrative activities and student registration.
- Provide students with online access to examination results and class timetables.
- Simplify student and user-account management.
- Improve information accessibility and the overall user experience.
- Ensure reliable and persistent data storage.

## Main Features

### Public Interface
- Public-facing institutional website.
- Information about ESSAT and its educational programmes.
- Online pre-registration form for prospective students.

### Student Area
- Secure user authentication.
- Access to personal student information.
- Examination results consultation and PDF downloads.
- Class timetable consultation and downloads.
- Password recovery functionality.

### Administrator Dashboard
- Administrator authentication.
- Student account management.
- User and administrator management.
- Pre-registration management and account activation.
- Management of student-related information and academic documents.
